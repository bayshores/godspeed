## 5.4 Background jobs

Every job that needs a model runs as a session of its own on the agent lessons run on (the learner's picked agent, or Claude Code before a pick): reading a file or a lesson for facts, topic grouping, a space check, a deadline's Ask, a cheat sheet, a mock, a plugin's question, a lesson's PDF reading, research or demo, a lesson's summary when it is shortened ([5](05-one-chat-turn.md#compaction)), and a lesson's notes for the notes folder ([8.1.6](08.1.6-vault-notes.md)). The teacher panel's chat never sees a job.

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `OneOff` | `engine::turn::job_session` | One job: its instructions, its input, godspeed's tools for it (a `ToolHost`), its folder, its model-call cap, its abort and what hears it run |
| `run_once` | `engine::turn::job_session` | Opens a session that is not kept, sends the input, runs godspeed's tools and keeps the agent's last text as what the job made. The job ends with the agent's turn (after one more turn when the job asks for it), at Stop, past its model-call cap (the agent is stopped and the text so far is the result), or after 10 minutes with no word from the agent |
| `JobAgent` | `agent::subagents::job_agent` | What every job runs on, with its priority: `Waited` (the learner asked for it on a page, or an open lesson waits on it) or `Own` (godspeed's own upkeep) |
| `AppJobAgent` | `services::jobs::job_agent` | Runs the job on the lessons' agent (`folder_agent`: `agent` in `data/agent-folder.json`, or Claude Code before a pick) once the pool gives it a permit, never waiting for a pick, in a new empty folder (`<tmp>/godspeed-jobs/<id>`, removed after) or in the folder the job names (a cheat sheet's) |
| The pool | `agent::subagents::bg_llm_semaphore`, held in `JobsState` | 3 permits for all jobs together. Waited jobs go before godspeed's own, first in, first out within each. A waiting job that is stopped leaves the queue |
| Submit jobs | `agent::extraction::submit_agent` | A job that ends by calling its submit tool (findings, topic groups, a deadline's answer). A valid submit ends the job; a refused one goes back to the agent with the reason; a turn that ends without one gets one reminder turn; a job that never submits fails |

Lessons and jobs share the agent's runtime (`services::app::agents`) but never a session, so a lesson's turn and its jobs run at once.

### Permission questions

A job's agent may ask to use one of its own tools (a command, a file outside its folder). The question shows as the lessons' Allow and Deny card on the page that started the job, until the learner answers or the job ends (`services::jobs::asks`).

| Job | Where the card shows |
|---|---|
| A lesson's PDF reading, research or demo | That lesson's desk: `approvals` on `GET /api/chats/{id}/work`, answered at `POST /api/chats/{id}/approvals/{approvalId}` |
| A cheat sheet | The sheet's chat, under its working line: `approvals` on the sheet's view, answered with `{action: "approval", id, allow}` |
| Anything else (godspeed's own upkeep, a deadline's Ask, a mock, a plugin's question) | Nowhere: nobody watches it, so the question is denied |

### What the learner sees

A demo's or PDF reading's newest activity line is saved on its job record (`activity`). The PDF reader's lines are also steps on its lesson's desk at the page it reads (`turn_work::job_activity`, [7.1](07.1-while-the-teacher-works.md)). Background work is shown under the teacher's name.

### Catch-up

At the server's start, `services::turns::agent_catch_up::catch_up` reads the lessons whose last stretch never reached the graph and queues the notes of lessons left with new answers. Their jobs run like any other.
