## 8.1.7 Follow's slip and the lit marks

While Follow is on and the teacher works, a slip at the page's foot says what it does. The server keeps the teacher's round of work ([8.1.2](08.1.2-the-work-loop.md#the-teachers-round-of-work)); the window draws it. The code is in `src/frontend/components/teacher/`.

### The timeline

`TeacherProvider` owns the round (`use-teacher-work.ts`: `GET /api/teacher-work`, read again on `teacher-work`). `timeline-model.ts` makes one timeline of it, oldest first:

| Row | From | A click |
|---|---|---|
| A read or a note | The round's steps | A file read's link opens that passage in the source pop-up (`CiteDialog`); others open nothing |
| A change | The activity log's entries since the round began, the agent's own and not a background check's (`isAgentEntry`) | Goes to it with `go` |

No word is the browser's own except "Thinking", "Working", "Paused while you type", "Paused while this is open", "Done, N steps", "N steps" and "Go to {page}".

### When the slip shows

`follow-slip-model.ts` decides, `use-follow-slip.ts` keeps its clock and the ended round's rows.

- Follow is on and the round is live, or ended less than 3 s ago with at least one row (`DONE_MS`), or ended with its list open.
- Never on a lesson, a mock or a cheat sheet (`slipPlace`), nor while the teacher's open panel fills the window. The welcome screen covers it.

### What it says

One line: a mark, the words, the count ("4 steps", `font-count`) and a chevron while there is a list to open.

| State | Mark | Words |
|---|---|---|
| A row came less than 1.2 s ago (`FRESH_MS`) | The pencil, breathing | That row's words |
| Live, nothing new | The pencil, breathing | "Thinking" |
| Follow waits (`TourPause`) | The pencil, still | "Paused while you type" or "Paused while this is open", and a "Go to {page}" key for the change it waits to show, else an open the window held |
| Ended | A check | "Done, 6 steps", no count |

- The whole line is one button. It opens upward into the round's rows the line does not say, newest at the bottom, eight in view, each with a check and dimmer. Only a change and a file read are keys, with a chevron.
- While the slip is up, the panel's head, the peek's status row, the strip's label and the Settings tile say only "Working" (`agentDoing` takes "Working" for the round), and the strip's one announcer says the slip's words. While the slip shows a pause, the peek draws no "Go to".
- Follow waits only while a change is queued for the tour. A click on a "Go to" goes at once: a visit the learner asked for never waits for Follow.

### Where it sits

- Mounted once in `app-shell.tsx`, outside `main` and the magnified page, at z 35: over the page, under the docked panel, the strip, the peek, the chain table and dialogs.
- Fixed from the window's left edge to the open panel's left edge (`--lh-dock-width`, its width whether it pushes the page or lies over it) plus `--lh-strip`, so the strip and the slip read as one frame; the strip's dark curves into it at that corner.
- The panel reports how much of the page is in view beside it. With less than 240 points (`slipFits` in `follow-slip-model.ts`) there is no slip: the panel's screen shows the work.
- In a narrow room the line's words end in "…" but the count and the chevron always show, and the "Go to" key drops under the line. The list's rows wrap; they are never cut.
- The page makes room as for the docked panel: `main`'s foot padding reads `--lh-follow-push` (the line's 44 points, more while the "Go to" key sits under it) and glides with main's padding. The room goes back the moment the slip starts to leave.
- On the Graph, `use-room.ts` leaves that padding out of the room's height and reports it as `foot`: the picture keeps its size and camera and is cut at the lifted foot, while the foot line and the Graph's own slip ride up above Follow's.

### Motion

It rises from past the window's foot by its height and leaves the same way (`use-slip-travel.ts`, `slip-travel-model.ts`, shared with the Graph slip), held by presence until it has left. Its words and count step in again when they change (`.lh-block-in`), its list folds open (`useFold`, `.lh-collapse`). Up with the window's first read of a live round, it is simply there and the page's foot does not glide. With reduced motion nothing moves.

### Lit marks

What the tour lights stays lit. `use-tour.ts` keeps `lit`, each target's key ("deadline:ddl_1") with the page it was lit on (`lit-model.ts`); `TeacherProvider` draws one `<style>` with a rule per key (`[data-teacher-target="…"]`, the tint and the 2-point edge, drawn as inset shadows so they lie over a sheet's face too), so a row drawn again keeps its mark and no page changes.

| A mark leaves when | How |
|---|---|
| The pointer goes over its thing | One `pointerover` listener for the page |
| The learner is on another page | Marks lit on other pages go |
| The next round of work begins | All go |

A mark fades in (`--lh-dur`) and out (`--lh-dur-fast`) and is then dropped; with reduced motion it changes at once. A burst lights every changed thing on its page. A change whose thing is not on the page lights nothing; the slip has named it. The targets are `data-teacher-target` on the things a change names, each on the element that paints the thing's face (a week block, a sheet, a row), never on a wrapper whose child paints over the mark; a deadline's page has one on its head. Where a count block or a file's badge holds a face's left side, the tint shows and the edge lies under the block. A tab on the top bar's dark takes the bar's ink as its tint.
