## 7.1 While the teacher works

While a lesson turn runs, the foot of the lesson text shows the desk (`src/frontend/components/lesson/agent-at-work.tsx`, paper in `page-stage.tsx`), where the reply will appear. `wait-model.ts` holds its pure rules. The top bar's pill says who works and whether an agent took the turn, so the desk shows no words: no "Thinking", no teacher's name, no clock.

### States

| State | When (`waitView`) | The learner sees |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting | A turn runs and no agent teaches (`waitingForAgent`) | A pile of paper in a 340-point well, pale and still, Stop under it, and the start key beside Stop when no agent will come by itself (below) |
| Working | An agent teaches | The same pile, floating slowly |
| Split | The reply in progress has a `work_plan` or a step | The plan on the left (done items checked, a spinner on the current one, the newest step alone under it; a plan longer than six rows folds its done items into one row with their count), the paper on the right. A file the teacher opens lies on top and the pages drift through it once |
| None | No turn runs, or the reply's text has arrived | Nothing |

The top sheet is the first of these (`deskPaper`), shown at page 1 (`GET /api/reading`, `pdfPageUrl`):

1. The file the teacher has open in this reply (the split desk's page stage).
2. A homework lesson's assignment file.
3. The file this lesson's teacher read last, in any reply (`lastRead`).
4. The newest study file on the space's card that the desk can show (text or PDF, not Word, PowerPoint or EPUB). The card lists files in the order they were added.
5. Blank paper.

Under it lie one, two or three edges for one, five or twenty files on the space's card, the lesson's first message. Narrow, the split desk is one column (under 560 points of desk) and the paper goes (under 360).

The steps come from the reply's tool calls (`agentWork`), one plain line each: "searched your files for …" (`search_space_files`), "checked what you already studied" (`search_graph`), "read lines 1 to 21 of Week 7 Graphs.md" (`read_passage`, also "page 3" of a PDF). A passage's file and place come from the head line of its result, or before that from the search that found it; its path is the card file whose path holds the file's id (a copied file) or, else, the card file of that name.

The start key (`StartAgentAction`, "Turn on {teacher}") stands beside Stop on the waiting desk when no agent is here (`noAgentHere`) and godspeed is not starting one: a lesson input starts the agent last started here by itself (§8.1.1, Hidden starts), and the key stays away while that start runs (`waking`) or the teacher's terminal starts. It also stays away while the teacher's panel shows its own start key (`panelStarts`).

### Title echo

A new lesson sends its title as its first message. When that message only repeats the title (`titleEcho`, spaces aside), it shows no bubble; files sent with it still show.

### The landing

The outside agent's reply arrives whole. In order:

1. The desk fades out (110 ms, `useLastWhileClosing`). The messages are held as they stood (`heldBack`, `deskMark`), also the words of a reply that grows the message an answered question is in, so the desk and the reply never stack, and the page holds still (`LessonScroll`'s rest returns the current place while `[data-desk-closing]` is on the page).
2. The reply's text mounts and its blocks step in top to bottom, 30 ms apart (`.lh-set`). The page is placed before it is drawn, from the resize it makes. With no "Your turn", a reply longer than the window rests at its start (`restingTop`).
3. The step line, when this reply wrote the first plan, steps in with the text.
4. "Your turn" steps in last, 240 ms after it mounts.

After an answer or a skip, "Your turn" fades out (110 ms) while the lesson text and it stay side by side, and the desk rises in at the foot of the text. A lesson reopened mid-turn shows the desk at once, with no motion.

### Stop and errors

Stop fades the desk out; text already written stays. When a first reply was stopped before the teacher wrote anything (`nothingWritten`), "Start again" at the foot runs the turn again from the record (the `continue` input). An error keeps its line with Try again in "Your turn" instead. The stuck line ("{teacher} took this N minutes ago") shows in "Your turn" while the desk keeps working.
