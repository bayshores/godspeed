## 7.1 While the teacher works

While a lesson turn runs, the foot of the lesson text shows the pencil (`src/frontend/components/lesson/pencil-line.tsx`), where the reply will begin, and Stop at the end of the same row. Once the teacher has a plan or takes a step in this reply, the desk (`src/frontend/components/lesson/agent-at-work.tsx`, paper in `page-stage.tsx`) takes the pencil's place, above Stop. `wait-model.ts` holds its pure rules. The strip says who works and whether an agent took the turn, so the pencil and the desk show no words: no "Thinking", no teacher's name, no clock.

### States

| State | When (`deskState`) | The learner sees |
|---|---|---|
| None | No turn runs, or the reply's text has arrived | Nothing |
| Pencil | A turn runs and the teacher has no plan or step yet in this reply | A pencil writes a line of pixel handwriting (`pencil-line-model.ts`), lifts, and writes it again over the faded line, so the row is never empty once it has written. While no agent has taken the turn it lies still, with no ink, and the start key stands before Stop when none will come by itself (below). With reduced motion: the written line and the pencil, still |
| Plan | The reply in progress has a `work_plan` or a step | The plan (done items checked; the current one on a tile with the spinner and the newest step alone under it; a plan longer than six rows folds its done items into one row with their count) |
| Reading | The teacher opened a text file or PDF in this reply (`read_passage`, `read`) | The plan on the left and the file on the right, in a well that fills the lesson text's sheet down to its foot (at least 420 points), with no label, since the step names the file. The paper stands straight and still. Its pages drift through the file once, then settle on the lines read: those get the highlighter as one bar in the margin, drawn down, and the lines around them fade |

Narrow, the desk is one column (under 560 points of desk) and the paper goes (under 360). Word, PowerPoint and EPUB files show no paper.

The steps come from the reply's tool calls (`agentWork`), one plain line each: "searched your files for …" (`search_space_files`), "searched your knowledge graph" (`search_graph`), "read lines 1 to 21 of Week 7 Graphs.md" (`read_passage`, also "page 3" of a PDF). A passage's file and place come from the head line of its result, or before that from the search that found it; its path is the card file whose path holds the file's id (a copied file) or, else, the card file of that name.

The start key (`StartAgentAction`, "Turn on {teacher}") stands beside Stop while the turn waits for an agent and none is here (`noAgentHere`) and godspeed is not starting one: a lesson input starts the agent last started here by itself (§8.1.1, Hidden starts), and the key stays away while that start runs (`waking`) or the teacher's terminal starts. It also stays away while the teacher's panel shows its own start key (`panelStarts`).

### Title echo

A new lesson sends its title as its first message. When that message only repeats the title (`titleEcho`, spaces aside), it shows no bubble; files sent with it still show.

### The landing

The outside agent's reply arrives whole. Its words often reach the page a moment before its turn ends; under the pencil they are held until it does, 400 ms at most (`useReplyHold`), so the words take the place of the pencil and Stop in one step. In order:

1. The desk fades out (110 ms, `useLastWhileClosing`). The messages are held as they stood (`heldBack`, `deskMark`), also the words of a reply that grows the message an answered question is in, so the desk and the reply never stack, and the page holds still (`LessonScroll`'s rest returns the current place while `[data-desk-closing]` is on the page).
2. The reply's text mounts and prints line by line, top to bottom, one block after the other, Sources last (`usePrintIn`, `print-in-model.ts`): 60 ms a line, and a long reply faster so none takes more than 900 ms. Its first line shows at once, where the pencil wrote. A block that is not lines of text (a table, a figure, code, display math, a cited page) appears whole. The lesson's two sheets glide to their new heights (`useSheetGlide`, `sheet-glide-model.ts`: 110 to 320 ms by how far the edge travels), the text's first, "Your turn" when its contents swap. The page holds still while they do and glides to its rest once, 140 ms after the last change (`HOLD_MS` in `LessonScroll`). With reduced motion the reply is just there, the sheets change size at once, and the page is placed before it is drawn. With no "Your turn" drawn (a narrow room, nothing waiting), a reply longer than the window rests at its start (`restingTop`).
3. The step line, when this reply wrote the first plan, steps in with the text.
4. "Your turn" steps in last, 240 ms after it mounts, when this is the lesson's first reply. Later its contents change inside the sheet: the old fade out (110 ms) and the new rise in.

After an answer or a skip, the lesson text and "Your turn" stay side by side, and Stop shows at the foot of the text. "Your turn" keeps the question just answered, dimmed and not pressable (`AnsweredTurn` in `src/frontend/components/lesson/answered-turn.tsx`): its choices with the learner's pick marked and the verdict chip beside it (Right, Wrong; a prediction or an ask has none), "Skipped" for a skip, or the "Didn't know" chip alone. It reads the answer's record (`lastAnswered` in `turn-next-model.ts`), and stays until the next question, problem or next state arrives, which swaps in with the fade. After anything else the learner did (a line, Keep going, the next problem), the last line saying what is next stays, dimmed, as before (`turnShown`). The desk rises in at the foot of the text once the teacher plans or takes a step. A lesson reopened mid-turn shows the desk at once, with no motion.

While a turn runs, and until its reply has landed (400 ms), the lesson page never gets shorter (`holdPageHeight` in `lesson-scroll-hold.ts` holds the height of the sheets' column as a minimum). WebKit lays the page out between a row leaving and the row that replaces it arriving, as when the desk takes Stop and the reply's steps or leaves before the reply, and the browser would pull the page up by the gap. Under each other, the text's sheet takes the room the hold keeps (as when "Your turn" loses a key), so "Your turn" still ends at the window's end and no empty page stands under it. When the lesson's window changes size (the app's window, the Text size, the teacher's panel) the hold starts over from the column's height there, so no empty room is left to scroll into.

The page follows new content at once (`resize="instant"`), except while its sheets glide (above). A new resting place glides there, on a firm spring that arrives in about a third of a second with no slow creep at its end.

### Stop and errors

Stop fades the desk out; text already written stays. When a first reply was stopped before the teacher wrote anything (`nothingWritten`), also one saved empty, the lesson text says so in one line and "Start again" in "Your turn" runs the turn again from the record (the `continue` input); "Continue" waits for the teacher's first words. An error keeps its line with Try again in "Your turn" instead. The stuck line ("{teacher} took this N minutes ago") shows in "Your turn" while the desk keeps working.
