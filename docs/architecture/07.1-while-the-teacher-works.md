## 7.1 While the teacher works

While a lesson turn runs, the desk stands at the end of the lesson text's newest step, with no box around it (`src/frontend/components/lesson/agent-at-work.tsx`, the paper in `page-stage.tsx`), from the turn's first moment until the teacher writes, and again whenever it goes back to work after writing (`useTeacherWriting`). `wait-model.ts` holds its pure rules. The desk reads `GET /api/chats/{id}/work` (`useLessonWork`, read again on `lesson-work` and `agent-changed`; the lesson's loader reads it first, so a lesson opened mid-wait shows its desk at rest). While the desk speaks, the strip, the peek's status row and the panel's head say only "Working".

### What the desk shows

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Sentence | One line in plain words, by why the turn runs (`deskHead`, the server's `cause`): "{teacher} is getting your first step ready." (no teacher words yet), "is reading your answer.", "is checking your work." (a problem's work), "is answering your question." (a question or a steer), "is writing the next step." (Continue, a finished job), "is trying again." (Try again), "is getting the problem ready." (a homework problem opened). Between the learner's send and the server's first word the line is kept empty, so nothing moves when the words come. |
| Rows | With a plan (`work_plan`), its items (done checked, the current one, the next ones), the newest step under the current item, a plan longer than six rows folded into "N done". Without one, the steps, the newest current, the oldest folded past six. Before the first step, the current row is the pencil writing a line of pixel handwriting (`pencil-line.tsx`). The rows' marks stand under the sentence's first letter. The current row lies on one tile, a sheet that hangs out past the marks and slides to the next current row (`useLitMarker`); its mark is the pencil writing a few short strokes. Done rows carry a check. |
| Paper | One sheet of paper lying on the page with no box around it, and no file name on it: the step beside it names the file (`deskPaper`). It shows the place the teacher read last in a file that has pages; before any read, the first page of the first of the teacher's files that has them, lying still (the server lists the lesson's picked files, else the deadline's assignment and covered files, else the space's study then info files). When the teacher reads, the pages drift through the file once, then settle on the place read; in a text file the lines read get the highlighter as one bar in the margin. A file the teacher turns to later fades in on the same sheet. The sheet is one letter page tall; the desk takes the page's blank room, and in a room shorter than a page the sheet is shorter too, its page cut at the sheet's foot, so the paper never makes the lesson scroll while the room has 320 points for it (180 under the rows in a narrow desk). Word, PowerPoint and EPUB files have no pages to show. |
| No paper | No file with pages: the rows keep their place under the sentence. |
| Allow cards | Under the rows, a card for each thing the agent waits for the learner to allow (`approvals`, `approval-card.tsx`). The server words it (`crates/core/src/session/approval_card.rs`): one sentence in the teacher's name on what it wants ("Claude wants to run this command.", "wants to change this file.", "has a question."), and what it is about (the command, the file's name, the site, the search, the tool or the question) as its own slip of paper under it, a command in the agent's own type. Deny and Allow post `POST /api/chats/{id}/approvals/{approvalId}`. A card opens its room under the rows as it fades in, and folds shut as it fades out once answered, or denied by Stop or the turn's end. A permission question brings the desk back while the teacher's words show. A card that comes below the window has the page glide down once until it shows (`onAsk` in `use-page-rest.ts`), unless the learner scrolled away; the page then rests there until the next turn or question. While one waits, the strip says "needs you" (the `asking` field of `lesson-work`), and a click on it opens that lesson, or shows the card when the lesson is open. |
| Foot | The lines typed to the teacher that wait for it. |

Stop is not on the desk: it stands in the writing row, after the field's own keys ([§5.2](05.2-the-lesson-page.md), The writing row).

The steps are the server's words (`crates/core/src/session/teacher_steps.rs`), one per thing the teacher does while it holds the lesson's turn: its godspeed reads, the tools its reply runs, PDF progress, and its own web searches, file reads and commands (§8.1.7). The lesson text hides the cards of the tool calls the steps stand for while the desk shows (`deskCalls`). With reduced motion the pencil and pages lie still. The rows stand left and the stack right; narrow, the desk is one column (under 440 points of desk) with a short band of paper under the rows, and the paper goes under 360.

### Title echo

A new lesson sends its title as its first message. When that message only repeats the title (`titleEcho`, spaces aside), it shows no plate; files sent with it still show.

### The reply as it is written

The agent's words reach the page as it writes them (`message_update` on the turn's stream, at most every 100 ms), with the writing mark at their end. In order:

1. The first words wait under the desk 400 ms at most (`useReplyHold`), so a reply whose turn ends at once takes the place of the desk in one step. Then the desk fades out (110 ms, `useLastWhileClosing`). The messages are held as they stood (`heldBack`, `deskMark`), also the words of a reply that grows the message an answered question is in, so the desk and the words never stack, and the page holds still (`usePageRest`'s target returns the current place while `[data-desk-closing]` is on the page).
2. The words show where the desk's sentence stood and grow in place; Stop stays in the writing row. The text's newest step and the turn's task glide to their new heights as the text grows (`useSheetGlide`, `sheet-glide-model.ts`: 110 to 320 ms by how far the edge travels). The page holds still while they do and glides to its rest once, 140 ms after the last change (`HOLD_MS` in `use-page-rest.ts`). Once the turn has words (`data-turn-wrote`), the page rests where the finished reply will: with no task in the page (none drawn, or a problem pinned over the text), a reply longer than the window rests at its start (`restingTop`), so nothing moves when the turn ends. With reduced motion the parts change size at once.
3. A step after the words (one of godspeed's tools or one of the agent's own) or a permission question brings the desk back under them, rising in; new words send it away again, as in 1.
4. When the turn ends, the step line, when this reply wrote the first plan, steps in. The turn (its task and the writing row) steps in last, 240 ms after it mounts, when this is the lesson's first reply. Later the task's contents swap in place (`TurnSwap`): the old fade out (110 ms) and the new rise in.

The text checked at the end of each message (`check_cites`) replaces what was shown live.

After an answer or a skip, the lesson text and the turn stay as they are laid out, and Stop shows in the writing row. Beside the text in a wide room (section 5.2, The book), the right page keeps the task just finished, quiet and not pressable (`heldTask` in `turn-next-model.ts`). A question stays as it stood when it was sent (`AnsweredTurn` in `src/frontend/components/lesson/answered-turn.tsx`): its words in the quieter ink with its kind tag under them, its choices as the same plates, the learner's pick on a quiet plate (the bright ink plate is only for a pick that can still change) with the verdict chip on it (Right, Wrong, on a graded quiz only), his own words or code under them; "Skipped" for a skip, or the "Didn't know" chip alone. A problem is drawn dimmed as its statement or its page picture, with "Discarded" under it when it was. The task stays through the teacher's reply about it and until the lesson goes on; the next question or problem swaps in with the fade. In one column no finished task is kept: the answered question is its one line in the text at once, and the task under the text stays empty while the teacher writes. The desk rises in at the foot of the text when the turn starts. A lesson reopened mid-turn shows the desk at once, with no motion, and so does a lesson that sends its first message as it opens (its desk is part of the opening).

While a turn runs, and for 400 ms after it ends, the lesson page never gets shorter (`holdPageHeight` in `lesson-scroll-hold.ts` holds the height of the page's column, `.lh-lesson-column`, as a minimum). WebKit lays the page out between a row leaving and the row that replaces it arriving, as when the desk takes the reply's steps or leaves before the reply, and the browser would pull the page up by the gap. When the lesson's window changes size (the app's window, the Text size, the teacher's panel) or an open problem is pinned over the text or leaves it (`pinned` in `usePageRest`), the hold starts over from the column's height there, so no empty room is left to scroll into. Nothing glides in a frame where its window changed (`useSheetGlide`): a part gliding from its old height would be measured by the hold and kept.

The page follows new content at once (`resize="instant"`), except while its parts glide (above). A new resting place glides there, on a firm spring that arrives in about a third of a second with no slow creep at its end.

### Stop and errors

Stop fades the desk out; text already written stays. When a first reply was stopped before the teacher wrote anything (`nothingWritten`), also one saved empty, the lesson text says so in one line and "Start again", the main key at the end of the writing row, runs the turn again from the record (the `continue` input); "Continue" waits for the teacher's first words. An error keeps its line with Try again over the writing row instead. The stuck line ("{teacher} took this N minutes ago and hasn't answered.", with its own key) shows over the writing row while the desk keeps working.
