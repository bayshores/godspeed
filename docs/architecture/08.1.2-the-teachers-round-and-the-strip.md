## 8.1 The outside-agent boundary: the teacher's round and the strip

### Opening a page from the terminal

A space or lesson an agent starts from godspeed's teacher terminal (not an agent outside godspeed; the shell says which terminal is the teacher's) opens in the window even with Follow off. `start_lesson`'s answer says the learner follows the lesson in the godspeed window.

Such a change is marked `open` in the activity log, and so is every `open_page` call, from any agent ([08.1.3](08.1.3-agent-tools-and-notes.md#opening-a-page)). An open is never merged with an earlier one, so asked twice it opens twice. The window (`teacher/use-tour.ts`) goes there with Follow on or off, and the teacher panel steps aside as for a click. While the learner has unsent lesson text, a mock runs or a dialog of theirs is open (`openWaits`; the panel's own Activity list doesn't count), the window stays and keeps the open as a held "Go to" in the strip's peek, shown with Follow on too, until its page is reached, a newer open replaces it or 10 minutes pass (`heldStill`, `GO_OFFER_MS`). Only a click on that Go to, or on Follow's slip (its Go to, a change in its list), leaves held work.

### The teacher's round of work

What the teacher reads and notes while it works outside lessons, for Follow's slip ([8.1.7](08.1.7-follows-slip.md)) (`services::app::terminal_state`), in memory only: a restart loses it, an ended round keeps nothing. `GET /api/teacher-work` answers `{live, since, chatId, steps: [{id, at, text, link?}]}`, `chatId` being the teacher panel chat whose turn it is, null for an agent in a terminal (`live: false`, `since: null`, no steps without a round). Each start, step and end sends `teacher-work`.

| | When |
|---|---|
| Starts | A turn of a teacher panel chat begins (`turn_work::begin`, [8.1.8](08.1.8-the-teacher-panels-chat.md)). The learner's line reaches the teacher's terminal (the prompt hook, `chat_line`). An agent outside godspeed's terminal: its first counted call. A new start replaces the live round. |
| Ends | The chat's turn ends, however it ends (`turn_work::end`). The teacher's Stop hook. 20 s with no counted call from an agent outside godspeed's terminal (`OUTSIDE_QUIET_MS`). 5 minutes with no step (`ROUND_STALE_MS`). Its terminal ended. |

- **A chat's steps** are its desk's: each step `turn_work::note_step` puts on a teacher panel chat's desk (a godspeed tool's, `talk_call`, with its file link; one of the agent's own tools) goes to its round too, made once. The chat's turn reaches the round only through `services::app::teacher_round`, so the round can leave the terminal state without a change to the chat's code.
- **Whose calls count** (`server::mcp::teacher_work`, in `call_tool` before the tool runs): the teacher terminal's, or an agent's outside godspeed's terminals. Lessons and jobs run on sessions of their own ([5.3](05.3-the-agent-session.md), [5.4](05.4-background-jobs.md)), so none of their calls is here: a lesson's reads and the agent's own tools are steps on that lesson's desk ([7.1](07.1-while-the-teacher-works.md)). The agent's own tools count only from the teacher's terminal, since only godspeed's terminals run the folder's hook.
- **Words** (`core::session::teacher_steps`): each tool is a change (no step: the activity log names it) or a step: "Looking at CSE 21", "Searching CSE 21 for “due”", "Reading HW7_F26.pdf, page 1" (a page only when the call's place names it), "Searching Obsidian for “x”" ("your notes folder" when Obsidian doesn't know it). A test fails for an undecided tool. Cut like activity labels (`short_label`, 80 characters). A file read links `{spaceId, fileId, name, passage}` (or `range`) for the source pop-up.
- It keeps the last 40 steps (`STEPS_MAX` in `teacher_steps.rs`). Over by time, it reads as ended at once; one wake per round ends it and sends the event.

### The strip

The window shows who works (`src/frontend/components/work/`): a striped frame around a page while an agent changes it (a space page, a deadline, a cheat sheet; a lesson whose facts are saved has none, since the strip says so and its page does not change), the strip on every page, and the teacher's tile under Settings, Connection, under a headline naming the agent (its chip says whether it runs; beside the teacher's open panel, the tile leaves its chip and line to the panel's head). Background work is shown under the teacher's name.

The strip (`src/frontend/components/strip/`) is 28 points wide (`--lh-strip`), runs down the window's right edge under the top bar on every page inside the app shell (a mock exam too), and is made of the top bar's dark. It is the teacher's panel at its narrowest: the page ends where it starts. The top bar has no teacher control. From the top:

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Square | Breathes while the teacher works, else still; hollow only until the window has first read the agent's state |
| Name | The teacher's name, set down the strip |
| Arrow | Points at the page; outside a lesson it steps at the page once every 4 seconds; under the pointer the strip leans 6 points toward the page and the arrow keeps stepping that way. With reduced motion nothing steps, leans or slides |
| Marks, at its foot, only while they have something to say | On the highlighter, "needs you" (the teacher's screen waits for the learner, or a lesson's teacher waits for the learner to allow something: the teacher's screen first, then the lesson) or "your turn" (a reply waits in another lesson); the folded notices as a count; a deadline in its last 24 hours as a countdown (not on Home, not on that deadline's own page) |

The peek (`peek-sheet.tsx`, `peek-store.ts`) is a sheet that slides out of the strip's top, under the bar and over the page, up to 400 points wide. It comes out when the pointer rests 300 ms on the strip or keyboard focus is on it, and tucks back 250 ms after the pointer left both, at once on a press of the strip, on Esc, on a click on the page and on a page change. The strip's marks hide while it is out. Its rows, in order:

1. The teacher's status line (`teacherHead` in `teacher-panel.tsx`, the same words as the panel's head: what the teacher does now, else "<teacher> is on" while an agent is connected, else "Ready"); lit while the teacher needs the learner. A click does what a click on the strip does.
2. Each lesson whose reply waits: its title, "your turn" and an Open key.
3. Background work under way, by what it does ("Reading 06_Counting.pdf") with its count ("3 of 12").
4. The "Go to Files" key while the teacher changed another page and Follow is off or held.
5. "2 notices", which shows the folded notices under the rows.
6. A deadline in its last 24 hours: its name and countdown, which open the deadline (plain words on the lesson that works on it).

A click on the strip shows the panel's chat whose card waits to be allowed (`showChat`, so the chat's window comes forward while popped out); else opens the teacher's panel (`setPanelOpen`: on its chat, or on Activity alone while the chat is popped out into its own window), which slides out of the strip and docks left of it; else closes the open panel (`stripClick`). Beside the open panel the strip is the arrow, now pointing back at the edge, and the marks alone, and the peek lists only what the panel's head does not say. Follow itself is a key in the panel's head ("Following", "Follow"). The page's room is the window less the strip and the docked panel (`panelLayout`, `--lh-page-side`, `--lh-title`); a lesson plugin's window keeps clear of the strip.

A notice (update, restart, new files, saved rule) shows in the peek's sheet by itself for 12 seconds with its own title and close key, then folds into the strip's count; a notice with its own timer closes by itself instead (`notice-store.ts`). While the teacher's panel is open, and on a lesson page, where it would lie over the step line's keys, no notice shows by itself: it waits folded in the count, and a notice with its own timer starts its time when it is shown. Time stands still while the pointer or keyboard focus is on the sheet (a clicked key keeps focus but holds nothing), and after the hold a notice has at least 3 seconds and at most 12 seconds left. With no strip on screen (the first steps) the sheet floats in the window's top right corner.

The teacher is started from its own window, so its tile has no button. Its chip says "Teaching" while the teacher works on something, else "Running", and its line says what it works on, else "Nothing to do right now".
