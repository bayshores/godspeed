# 6.8 Gaps

A deadline that looks missing: how godspeed finds it, how the learner waves it off and what the teacher is told.

## Gaps

A gap is a deadline that looks missing (`core::spaces::space_gaps`). A space has at most one. A closed space, or one with fewer than 2 deadlines, has none. Every deadline counts, done or not.

| Gap | When | Expected day | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhythm | A kind (homework, quiz, project or exam, never interview) has 3 or more distinct due days. Their middle spacing `g` (two middles averaged and rounded) is 3 to 15 days. The newest, `L`, is before today and today is before `L + 2g` | `L + g`, then `L + 2g` once today is past `L + g` | `{space}:{newest deadline}:1` or `:2` |
| Nothing coming up | No rhythm gap, and no deadline is dated today or later. Up to 14 days after the newest deadline it is "Nothing coming up", after that "Is {space} over?" (`close`) | none | `{space}:{newest deadline}:recent` or `:over` |

- Of several rhythms, the one expected soonest wins.
- Finals week: no rhythm gap when the space's newest deadline is an exam after `L` and on or before `L + 2g`.
- The outline's name counts up the newest deadline's number ("HW6" gives "HW7?"), else it is the kind's word ("Homework?").
- A spacing of 6 to 8 days reads as "this week" and "None this week", others as "soon" and "None for now".
- A linked folder whose newest file came 7 or more days ago replaces the line of both "Nothing coming up" stages with "No new files from its folder since {day}."
- The server makes every word: `title`, `line`, `label`, `answer`, and `agent_line` for the teacher.
- Waving one off stores its key in `gapAsked`, replacing the space's older key (`services::spaces::gaps::answer_gap`). `open_gaps` leaves answered ones out. Adding the missing deadline ends the gap by itself.
- The teacher sees the unanswered gap as `outOfDate` in `read_space` and on the space's line of the "Where you left off" briefing.
- A deadline an agent adds that was due more than 7 days ago is saved done (`PAST_ADD_DAYS`), so old deadlines keep the rhythm's history and the learner has nothing to tick.
