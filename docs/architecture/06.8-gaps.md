# 6.8 Gaps

A deadline that looks missing: how godspeed finds it, how the learner waves it off and what the teacher is told.

## Gaps

A gap is a deadline that looks missing (`core::spaces::space_gaps`). A space has at most one. A closed space, or one with fewer than 2 deadlines, has none. Every deadline counts, done or not.

| Gap | When | Expected day | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhythm | A kind (homework, quiz, project or exam, never interview) has 3 or more distinct due days. Their middle spacing `g` (two middles averaged and rounded) is 3 to 15 days. The newest, `L`, is before today and today is before `L + 2g` | `L + g`, then `L + 2g` once today is past `L + g` | `{space}:{newest deadline}:1` or `:2` |
| Nothing coming up | No rhythm gap, and no deadline is dated today or later. Up to 14 days after the newest deadline it is "Nothing coming up", after that "Is {space} over?" (`close`) | none | `{space}:{newest deadline}:recent` or `:over` |

- Of several rhythms, the one expected soonest wins.
- Finals week: no rhythm gap when the space's newest deadline is an exam after `L` and on or before `L + 2g`.
- The outline's name counts up the newest deadline's number ("HW6" gives "HW7?"), else it is the kind's word ("Homework?").
- A spacing of 6 to 8 days reads as "this week" and "None this week", others as "soon" and "None for now".
- A linked folder whose newest file came 7 or more days ago replaces the line of both "Nothing coming up" stages with "No new files from its folder since {day}."
- The server makes every word: `title`, `line`, `label`, `answer`, and `agent_line` for the teacher.
- Waving one off stores its key in `gapAsked`, replacing the space's older key (`services::spaces::gaps::answer_gap`). `open_gaps` leaves answered ones out. Adding the missing deadline ends the gap by itself.
- The teacher sees the unanswered gap as `outOfDate` in `read_space` and on the space's line of the "Where you left off" briefing.
- A deadline an agent adds that was due more than 7 days ago is saved done (`PAST_ADD_DAYS`), so old deadlines keep the rhythm's history and the learner has nothing to tick.

## In the browser

The server sends each open gap on `GET /api/deadlines` as `gaps` (`Gap::to_json`, every word but `agent_line`). `use-open-deadlines.tsx` is the one owner: it reads them (`readGaps`), reloads when the day changes, and `answerGap` posts `{key}` to `POST /api/deadlines/{space id}/nothing-new`.

| Where | What shows |
|---|---|
| Every page | The gap's card (`gap-card.tsx`) in the strip's peek, one at a time, with "Update" (the teacher is asked "Update {space}"; the card leaves for that run), the server's answer key, and for "Is {space} over?" "Close space" instead of "Update". A rhythm gap's card is left out on Home and on its own space's Deadlines tab, where the outline shows it (`gap-card-model.ts`). |
| Home's week | Each open space's rhythm gap as an outline: a block with no fill, dashed in its space's color, named by `label` ("HW7?"), in the column of its `day` or in Later (`week-columns-model.ts`). It sorts and folds like a deadline with no time, before one due the same day. It is never the card's own pick. |
| Home's card | A click on the outline shows its gap form (`NextGap` in `next-card.tsx`): the same card drawn as its outline, the days left to `day` with no fill, `label` as the title, `line` under it, and the keys "Ask {teacher}" (main; the teacher is asked "Update {space}"), "Add it myself" (the space's Deadlines tab with Add a deadline filled in, `addDeadlineHref(seedFromGap(gap))`) and the server's answer. With no teacher to ask, "Add it myself" is the main key. Once the gap is gone the card shows the next thing again. |
| Deadlines tab | "Ask {teacher} to update", beside "Add a deadline", when the teacher can be asked. The active space's rhythm gap as an outline row among "Coming up", placed by its `day` (`comingRows`), with "Add it myself" (Add a deadline filled in) and the server's answer (`gap-row.tsx`). |
| Teacher's "Try asking" row | The update line first (`starter-model.ts`). |

A card waits while a lesson or mock is open.

### The outline row

`OutlineRow` (`space-page/outline-row.tsx`) draws a deadline that is not all there yet: dashes in its space's color where a row's sheet would be, its days left where a row's block is (when it has a day), its name, one line and its keys. The outline row and an interview waiting for its date (`waiting-deadline.tsx`) both use it. Add a deadline opens filled in from one `DeadlineSeed` (kind, name, date, files, and a waiting deadline's id): `seedFromWaiting` and `seedFromGap` make it, and `addDeadlineHref` carries a gap's kind, name and date to the Deadlines tab from Home.

The dashes are `.lh-dashed` (shared with a note page still being written) in `spaceLineColor`: the space color itself on a dark background, half ink on Paper (`--lh-space-line-mix`), so they show at 3:1 on the page and in a well.

### Motion

An outline or a deadline that joins the week or the Deadlines list after it first drew steps in (`useArrivals`, `JOINED`: `.lh-block-in` after `--lh-dur-fast`), so the real deadline appears in the outline's place once the outline has faded (an outline sorts before a deadline due at the same moment, so it keeps its place while it fades). An outline that leaves is held by `useLeavingList` and fades (`.lh-fade`, `data-state="closed"`). A page that first draws with an outline shows it at rest: the Deadlines tab waits for both the space's record and the open deadlines' read before it draws its lists. With reduced motion nothing moves and a leaving outline goes at once.
