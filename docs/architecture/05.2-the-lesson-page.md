## 5.2 The lesson page

The lesson is an open page (`LessonScroll` in `src/frontend/components/lesson/lesson-scroll.tsx`) in three places, top to bottom. How each part looks (sizes, plates, tags, widths) is in the Page layout rules of [ui-rules.md](../ui-rules.md#page-layout); this file says how the code places it.

| Place | What it holds | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Step line | A block per step, the current step's name and the count ("Step 2 of 5"); a click unfolds every step ([§5](05-one-chat-turn.md), The plan tree). Hidden while it draws nothing. | `PlanRow` in `plan-row.tsx` |
| The page | The lesson text and the turn's task. It scrolls in the lesson's window (`.lh-lesson-window`), whose top and foot fade over 24 points; the page's own padding is as tall, so at rest nothing is faded. | `ReadingPane` in `reading-pane.tsx`, the task of `YourTurn` in `your-turn.tsx` |
| Writing row | The field and every key that sends or moves the lesson on | The row of `YourTurn`, `LessonBox` in `lesson-box.tsx` |

### The lesson text

The text has no sheets or boxes. `stepSheets` (`step-sheets-model.ts`) groups it by step: a step starts at the first question or problem tagged with a plan step, at a step's opener, or at a problem in practice and homework. A step (`lesson-sheet`) is left out until something in it is drawn, and its heading (`sheet-step`) shows only when the page holds more than one step.

- The learner's own lines are plates at the right (`UserMessage` in `message-row.tsx`). An answered question is one line (`AnsweredCard`, [§5](05-one-chat-turn.md), Question tools). While the teacher works, the desk stands at the end of the newest step ([§7.1](07.1-while-the-teacher-works.md)).
- A task the turn shows is drawn once: its card, and the room around it, is left out of the text while the turn holds it (`ChatActionsContext.inTurn`, read in `message-row.tsx`). When the turn lets it go, the card opens in its place in 220 ms (`FiledCard` in `filed-card.tsx`) while the page moves by as much as it grows, so the lines under it hold still (`useFiledCardHold`).

### The turn

The turn (`YourTurn`, through `TurnSheet` in `turn-sheet.tsx`) is there for the whole lesson once the teacher has spoken or a turn runs, or earlier when it holds something to press (`turnDrawn` in `turn-next-model.ts`). It has two parts:

| Part | What it holds |
|---|---|
| The task (`.lh-turn-task`) | A waiting question (`QuestionDock`), the open problem (`OpenProblemPane`), the task the learner last finished (a question in `AnsweredTurn`, or the problem dimmed), the line that says what is next, and at the last topic the lesson's notes (`EndNotes`). With nothing to show it takes no room. |
| The writing row (`.lh-turn-row`) | The lines and keys over the field, the field and its keys (The writing row, below). |

The task's contents swap when what waits changes (`TurnSwap` in `turn-swap.tsx`): the old fade out and the new rise in, while the writing row stays still.

### Where the turn stands

`LessonScroll` draws the two parts into boxes of their own (`turnBox`) and moves the boxes between places (`moveTo`), so a line being typed, a pick and the keyboard are kept. A place is shown before a part moves in, since a part in a hidden place cannot take the keyboard back. A layout effect tells the lesson where the task stands (`TurnPlace` in `turn-place.ts`):

| Place | When | The task | The writing row |
|---|---|---|---|
| `beside` | A wide room (at least 1000 points, a container query on the lesson's own room) and the book open (below) | The right page, at its foot over the writing row | At the right page's foot |
| `tight` | As `beside`, with under 520 points of room under the step line (`KEEP_ROOM`) | As `beside`, but the finished task is not kept there: it is drawn in the text | As `beside` |
| `alone` | A wide room, and the turn is up while the lesson text draws nothing (a lesson that opens with a problem) | A page in the middle at the turn's width, at its foot over the writing row; it slides to its side (240 ms) when the text arrives | At that page's foot |
| `pinned` | One column, and an open problem with no question waiting | Pinned over the text, under the step line, up to 60% of the room | Pinned at the window's foot |
| `under` | Every other case | In the page under the text, at the page's foot, so it rests on the writing row | Pinned at the window's foot |

- The task rests on the writing row in every place but `pinned`. The page (`.lh-lesson-column`) is at least as tall as its window, and the turn (`.lh-turn-fit`) takes `margin-top: auto` under the text, or `justify-content: flex-end` as the right page, so blank room falls between the text and the task, never between the task and the row.
- A docked panel (the cheat sheet or the Document panel) and the teacher's panel take room from the lesson's room, so the page falls back to one column. A page drawn to text (a test) has no window, so the turn is drawn in the column there.
- Beside the text, the right page is sticky 24 points under the window's top and as tall as the window less 48 (the scroller is a size container, so `100cqh` is its height). The task's question or problem (`.lh-turn-scroll` in `turn-scroll.tsx`) scrolls in its own box when it is longer than the room over the writing row, and a cut end fades.
- A code question's editor takes the room left over the writing row (`.lh-turn-fit:has([data-kind="code"])`).

### The pinned problem

A problem with no choices to click, pinned over the text, folds once the learner has sent something, since a reply is coming (`problemFolded` in `turn-fold-model.ts`): it is cut to half of the room under the step line that the pin's other parts and the writing row leave (`foldRoom`, at least three lines). When its text marks the part being worked on (`data-part-current`), the box shows that part alone and holds still (`partSpan` in `turn-scroll.tsx`). "Whole problem" (`turn-whole`) opens it and then reads "Less"; it is there only while the problem is longer than its folded room, and the learner's choice holds until the next problem. The task glides to its new height when it folds (`glideSheetAround`).

A problem's keys ("Whole problem", the page key) sit in the corner of its plate (`.lh-turn-cornered`). A problem with a tag ("Problem 3" in practice) keeps them on a row over it, beside the tag, and so does a problem shown as its printed page, with As printed among them, since the page keeps the whole width that sets the size of its print.

### The book

In a wide room the lesson is a book with two states. The text's page is as wide in both (`--lh-text`, set on `.lh-lesson-room`), so no line wraps anew when it moves.

| State | When | What is drawn |
|---|---|---|
| Open | The teacher asks, a problem is open, or a finished task is kept (`bookOpen` in `turn-next-model.ts`) | The text on the left page, the turn on the right page (`.lh-lesson-pair`) |
| Shut | Nothing to work on | The text as one page in the middle (`.lh-lesson-closed`), the task under it, the writing row pinned at the window's foot as wide as that page |

- The right page keeps the task the learner last finished (`heldTask`: a question answered or skipped, a problem concluded or discarded), quiet and not pressable, in the places `beside` and `alone` only. It goes once he went on and the teacher's reply to that landed whole with words in it, or when no key to go on stands and no turn runs; a reply still on its way, stopped or failed keeps it. At the last topic the lesson's notes take its place when they come ([vault notes](08.1.6-vault-notes.md)).
- A question from earlier never opens the book. While the book is shut and the teacher works, a question or problem that streams in waits for the reply to land.

### The book's move

The book opens or shuts only when a reply has landed whole (`useBook` in `use-book.ts`): never while the teacher works, never while the desk is still leaving the text, never on a stopped or failed reply, and at most once per reply. Between two tasks nothing slides; the next task swaps in on the right page.

1. The page is laid out in its new state at once and takes its rest there in the same step (`placePage` in `use-page-rest.ts`). The landed reply is in the page at its full height and waits to print (`PrintWaitContext`).
2. The pieces are moved from where they stood (`useBookMove` in `use-book-move.ts`), with transforms and clips only:

   | Direction | Order |
   |---|---|
   | Opening | The text's page slides left while the turn slides right (240 ms), then the turn rises into the right page (220 ms) |
   | Shutting | The kept task fades from the right page (110 ms), the right page sinks to the window's foot (220 ms), then the two slide together (240 ms) |

3. The writing row moves between its places as a box (`moveTo`), so it keeps the keyboard and a line being typed.
4. The pages say when they stand (`rest`). The reply prints, and the glides (`useSheetGlide`) start again.

While the pages move, a scroll the browser makes by itself (what the page holds was lower for a moment) is put back where the page stood. With reduced motion, in one column, or when the room changes size, the page is laid out and placed at once.

### The writing row

Over the field stand, top to bottom: the stuck line, an error line with Try again, then "Stuck?" with three help keys while a question or problem waits, or "Try asking" (`lessonIdeas`: Draw it, Show a demo, Look it up, Quiz me; a job switched off in Settings takes its line with it) while nothing waits and the teacher is idle. Each key sends its words as a typed line would.

`LessonBox` draws the field (as tall as its text up to five lines, then it scrolls), the keys `lessonBox` in `lesson-box-model.ts` gives for what waits ([§5](05-one-chat-turn.md), Side questions, has the table), then the keys that end the row:

| Key | When |
|---|---|
| "Turn on {teacher}" (`StartKey` in `start-key.tsx`) | A turn waits for an agent, none is here, godspeed is not starting one and the teacher's panel does not show its own |
| Stop (`stop-turn`) | A turn runs, except while the teacher reads ahead at a stop |
| The next key (`next-problem-button`): "Continue", "Next problem", "Start problem N", "Done for today" or "Start again" | Nothing is asked and a next state has a key (`turnNext`), and no "My words" ask is up (then "Done for today" is one of the field's own keys) |

"Skip question" or "Discard problem" is the first line of the top bar's More (`LessonMore` in `lesson-more.tsx`) and asks first; a question that came through broken has its own Skip question key.

### Where the page rests

The page rests where `restingTop` (`lesson-scroll-model.ts`) says; `usePageRest` in `use-page-rest.ts` reads the page and moves it there. A rest is a whole number of points, so the page reaches it and stays.

| The task | Where the page rests |
|---|---|
| Beside the text (the column is a grid) | At its end |
| Not in the page (none drawn, or a problem pinned over the text) | At its end; a reply that landed and is longer than the window is read from its start |
| In the page (`under`, `alone`) | Where the task ends at the window's end. When that would hide the start of the newest thing, on that start: the learner's last message once the teacher writes back while the task stays open, else the part the learner is on when it sits over 60% of the window down the task, else the task's top. |

At rest, and not while the teacher writes, the page moves up as much as 48 points when the window's top edge would cut a line of text (`wholeLineTop`). It goes to its rest on open, when the teacher asks something new (`restKey`) and while the teacher writes; scrolled up, the jump key brings the learner back. How it holds still while a reply lands is in [§7.1](07.1-while-the-teacher-works.md), The landing.

### How it opens

A lesson opens at rest: after it first shows, nothing in it moves by itself.

| What the first drawing needs | Where it is read |
|---|---|
| The record, the lesson's jobs, the installed plugins (a plugin's word in the bar), the question from earlier that may show, a problem list and documents | The route's loader (`lessonLoader` in `routes/lesson.tsx`), so the old page stays until all are read |
| The unsent draft, the panel left open beside the lesson and its width | The state's initializer (`useLessonDrafts`, `useLessonDock`, `DockPanel`) |
| Where the turn stands, where the page rests, which problem parts are folded, the lesson's space lit in the bar | A layout effect, before the first frame is drawn |

What the lesson opened with never animates: a step line, a panel left open and the turn are just there (`TurnSwap` swaps at once while the task is first drawn); one that arrives later steps in, the turn 240 ms after the reply's text. A jobs read that failed in the loader is read again by the page.

Nothing takes its size late either:

- A glide starts from a part's first seen size (`useSheetGlide`), and the book's move does nothing as the page opens (`useBookMove`), so the page is placed once, before it is drawn.
- Where the page rests is taken again with each layout until the first frame is drawn (`usePageRest`), since where the turn stands is known only once the page is laid out.
- The top bar's tab strip marks its cut ends on the element (`data-cut`, set by `useTabStrip` in `tab-strip.ts`), not in state, so the lit tab has its final width in the layout that brings it into sight.
- A problem drawn as its page opens resting on the learner's part (`PagePiece` says where in a layout effect, and `restOnPart` in `turn-scroll.tsx` scrolls the task's box there); it glides only when he moves on to another part. A folded problem is first drawn whole and cut after, so its box takes its place on the part once cut.
- The field keeps its lowest height itself while its editor loads (`.lh-answer-box [data-testid="math-box"]`), so the row does not jump.
- A code block is drawn at its real height at once; Streamdown would leave one out of sight undrawn at a guessed 200 points.
