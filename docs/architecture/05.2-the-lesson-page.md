## 5.2 The lesson page

The lesson is one scrolling page (`LessonScroll` in `src/frontend/components/lesson/lesson-scroll.tsx`): the step line on its own sheet pinned on top, then the lesson text, one sheet per step (`stepSheets` in `step-sheets-model.ts`: a sheet starts at the first question or problem tagged with a plan step, at a step's opener, or at a problem in practice and homework; `ReadingPane` draws a sheet once something on it is drawn, with the step's number on its tab), and, once the lesson has started (the teacher has spoken or a turn runs), the "Your turn" sheet (`TurnSheet` in `turn-sheet.tsx`; `turnDrawn` in `turn-next-model.ts`). Both are reading surfaces with one margin (`.lh-reading`, see the Page layout rules in `docs/ui-rules.md`).

### The book

With room for both pages (a container query on the lesson's own room, at least 1280 points; a docked panel (the cheat sheet or the lesson's document) or the teacher's panel takes room away) the lesson is a book. `.lh-lesson-room` in `globals.css` sizes it inside the page's column, which every page shares (`.lh-page`, at most `--lh-page-w` wide inside `--lh-page-side` sides).

| State | When | What is drawn |
|---|---|---|
| Open | The teacher asks, a problem is open, or a finished task is kept (`bookOpen` in `turn-next-model.ts`) | The text on the left page, "Your turn" on the right page: 688 points where there is room, giving way first, down to 560, so the text keeps at least 632 |
| Shut | Nothing to work on | One page in the middle, as wide as the open book's left page (`--lh-text`), with "Your turn" a strip pinned under its window: the writing box and one row of keys |

- The text's page is the same width open and shut, so no line wraps anew when the book moves.
- Open, the text scrolls with the page and "Your turn" holds still under the step line, as tall as the window's room; the text's sheet is at least that tall and the page ends `--lh-gap` under the sheets (`.lh-turn-fit` and `.lh-text-fit`; the page's scroller is a size container, so `100cqh` is its height). Its question or problem (`.lh-turn-scroll`) takes the room that is left and scrolls in its own box when it is longer, so the box, Send, the Stuck? keys and the way out rest at the sheet's foot. At 688 the foot row is one line; narrower, the way out wraps to the right end of a second line.
- The right page is never empty. It holds the open task, or the task the learner last finished (`heldTask`: a question answered or skipped, a problem concluded or discarded), dimmed and not pressable, with the key that goes on. The kept task goes once he went on and the teacher's reply to that landed whole with words in it, or when no key to go on stands and no turn runs. A reply still on its way, stopped or failed keeps it. In a room under 460 points high (`KEEP_ROOM`: the steps unfolded in a low window) the box and keys leave a kept task no line to show, so it is drawn in the text as in a narrow room (`TurnPlace` is "tight").
- A task is drawn once: while the right page holds it, its card is left out of the text (`ChatActionsContext.inTurn`), and it is filed there when the right page lets it go. The card opens in 220 ms (`FiledCard` in `filed-card.tsx`) while the page follows its growth, so the lines under it hold still (`useFiledCardHold`).
- A question from earlier never opens the book: it shows wherever the box stands.
- Before the lesson starts the text is one centered column at a reading measure of 872 points, the lesson page's only width of its own.

### The book's move

The book opens or shuts only when a reply has landed whole (`useBook` in `use-book.ts`): never while the teacher works, never while the desk is still leaving the text, never on a stopped or failed reply, and at most once per reply. Between two tasks nothing slides; the next task swaps in on the right page.

1. The page is laid out in its new state at once and takes its rest there in the same step (`placePage` in `use-page-rest.ts`). The landed reply is in the page at its full height and waits to print (`PrintWaitContext`).
2. The pieces are moved from where they stood (`useBookMove` in `use-book-move.ts`), with transforms and clips only:

   | Direction | Order |
   |---|---|
   | Opening | The text's page slides left while the strip slides right (240 ms), then the strip rises into the right page (220 ms) |
   | Shutting | The kept task fades from the right page (110 ms), the right page sinks into the strip (220 ms), then the two slide together (240 ms) |

3. The writing box and the row under it stay in sight the whole way and keep the keyboard and a line being typed.
4. The pages say when they stand (`rest`). The reply prints, and the sheets' own glides (`useSheetGlide`) start again.

While the pages move, a scroll the browser makes by itself (what the page holds was lower for a moment) is put back where the page stood. With reduced motion, in a narrow room, or when the room changes size, the page is laid out and placed at once.

### Pinned in a narrow room

With less room, in a room at least 640 points high under the step line (`PIN_ROOM`), the text keeps the window and "Your turn" is pinned at the window's foot under it, for the whole lesson too (`.lh-turn-foot`). In a lower room it sits in the page under the text, as tall as what it holds, since a question with its box and keys would leave the text a sliver. Under the shut book's page it is pinned in any room while it is only its box and keys. It is one sheet in every layout: `LessonScroll` draws it into a box of its own and moves that box between the column and the foot, so a line being typed, the keyboard and a pick are kept (the foot is shown before the box moves in, since a box in a hidden place cannot take the keyboard back). Pinned (`TurnPlaceContext` tells `YourTurn`), it is only as tall as what the learner can do now: with nothing to answer, the box and the row under it with the key that goes on at the row's end, no head, and the line saying what is next over the box; while the teacher works, the box alone, since the lesson text shows the question just answered. A waiting question or an open problem is shown whole, with closer margins, and scrolls in its own box only when the window is too low to leave the text 88 points. The foot changing height changes the height of the text's window: the text keeps its place over the window's end, the page holds still while the sheet glides, and the move is told to the page's follower as a size change, never as the learner scrolling away.

### Alone

While "Your turn" is up and the lesson text draws nothing (a lesson that opens with a problem; `messageShows` in `message-row.tsx` says what draws), the text's sheet is left out: "Your turn" sits alone in the middle, at the width it keeps in the pair, and slides to its side when the text's sheet arrives (the learner's first line, or the teacher's first words).

### Where the page rests

The page rests at its end (`restingTop` in `lesson-scroll-model.ts`; `usePageRest` in `use-page-rest.ts` reads the page and moves it there). A rest is a whole number of points, so the page reaches it and stays. With "Your turn" pinned, a reply that landed and is longer than the window is read from its start. In the page of a narrow room (alone, or in a room too low to pin it), "Your turn" ends at the window's end; when it is taller than the window the page rests on the sheet's top, on the part the learner is on when that sits deep in the problem, or on the learner's last message once the teacher writes back while the problem stays open. Beside the text the page always rests at its end. At rest, and not while the teacher writes, the page moves up a few points when the window's top edge would cut a line of text (`wholeLineTop`). It goes there on open, when the teacher asks something new and while the teacher writes; scrolled up, the key in the corner (in the lesson text's side padding when the sheets are side by side) brings the learner back.

### How it opens

A lesson opens at rest: after it first shows, nothing in it moves by itself.

| What the first drawing needs | Where it is read |
|---|---|
| The record, the lesson's jobs, the installed plugins (a plugin's key in the bar), the question from earlier that may show, a problem list and documents | The route's loader (`lessonLoader` in `routes/lesson.tsx`), so the old page stays until all are read |
| The unsent draft, the panel left open beside the lesson and its width | The state's initializer (`useLessonDrafts`, `useLessonDock`, `DockPanel`) |
| Whether "Your turn" is pinned, where the page rests, which problem parts are folded, the lesson's space lit in the bar | A layout effect, before the first frame is drawn |

What the lesson opened with never animates: a step line that is there, a panel left open and the contents of "Your turn" are just there (`TurnSwap` swaps at once while the sheet is first drawn). A jobs read that failed in the loader is read again by the page.

Nothing takes its size late either:

- A sheet's first seen size is where its glides start from (`useSheetGlide`), and the book's move reports no rest as the page opens (`useBookMove`), so the page is placed once, before it is drawn, and no sheet glides to a size it already has.
- A problem drawn as its page opens resting on the part the learner is on (`PagePiece` says where in a layout effect); it glides there only when he moves on to another part.
- The answer box keeps its lowest height itself while its editor loads (`.lh-answer-lines [data-testid="math-box"]`), so what scrolls beside it keeps its place.
- A code block is drawn at its real height at once; Streamdown would leave one out of sight undrawn at a guessed 200 points.

### The writing box

The turn sheet has one writing box (`LessonBox` in `lesson-box.tsx`; `lessonBox` in `lesson-box-model.ts` decides its size, its keys, the way out at the title row's end (Skip question, Discard problem) and the "Stuck?" keys under it). With nothing waiting the row under the box is "Try asking" (`lessonIdeas`: Draw it, Show a demo, Look it up, Quiz me; a background job switched off in Settings takes its line with it), never both rows; each key sends its words as a typed line would. The box is there in every state and holds still while the rest of the sheet swaps (`TurnSwap` in `turn-swap.tsx`): everything the learner says to the teacher in a lesson is typed in it.
