# The theory of learning and teaching

The theory godspeed teaches by. The agent prompts in `crates/agent/prompts/` are a compressed form of it, so read this before you change how the teacher teaches.

## 1. Two knowledge states

- **Current understanding** is what the learner holds: what they accept as obviously true, self-evident and safe to build on.
- **Goal understanding** is what the learner wants to hold but doesn't yet. It is often hard to articulate, and pinning it down is part of defining it.

Measuring brackets the current understanding (what is held, where it ends) and makes the goal concrete. Teaching moves the learner from the first state to the second. In a study session the teacher gets each topic's standing from the learner's past answers. A fact that is only in the learner's files, with no answer from the learner, does not count as held. In an older teach lesson a prober measures first.

## 2. Learning is the goal feeling obviously true

A session ends not in recall or coverage but in the **goal understanding feeling obviously true**. A fact that can be recited but isn't self-evident is memorized, and memorized facts rot.

This is also how retention works. The brain commits fully to a fact only when it is safe to lock in (nothing more fundamental might later contradict it) and connected (held by its relations, not rehearsal). Feeling obviously true *is* the felt experience of both.

## 3. The click: loose facts collapsing into generators

The payoff is not the final fact arriving but what happens to knowledge already held. Loose facts, held as "that's just how it is", collapse into a structure generated by a few base facts. Each is seen to follow from something more fundamental, and the pile becomes a derivation.

This is knowing why instead of knowing that: the strongest understanding, and the clearest felt evidence that structure was built. The goal understanding (Section 2) is the end state of the same thing: obvious because the structure generates it.

## 4. Knowledge is structure

Understanding is a graph: **propositions as nodes, connections as edges**. Connections and reasoning steps are themselves facts that can be stated, examined and held. A proposition connected to nothing is fragile. One nested inside a structure is self-preserving. All of teaching is constructing this structure.

## 5. Teaching is asserting facts in an order, one reasoning step at a time

Teaching exposes the learner to a fact. A lesson is **facts, in the right order, each presented in the clearest possible way.**

The rule for order: **move one reasoning step at a time.** Start from what the learner holds (obviously true by definition). Attach each new fact to it by a *single* step, small enough to understand at once. The new fact inherits the obviousness of what it follows from. The session is a chain of small "of course" moments ending at the goal.

Small steps make errors surface at once. A gap or misconception shows the moment it is stepped on, not three nodes later when everything built on it is corrupted, so the failure is precisely localized. Strengthen or repair the weak spot *before continuing*. Never build on anything not yet held as obviously true.

## 6. Foundations

Some needed propositions cannot be derived from anything held. They need **foundational nodes**, the bottom of the dependency tree. A foundation is accepted *as-is*, so make it **unconditionally true**: simple enough to take at face value, no caveats or hedging.

A foundation can also be **motivated** by a problem, question or condition that demands it. Motivation is not derivation: it eases acceptance without making the fact follow. A fact that answers a felt question is easier to hold as self-evident than one from nowhere.

Derive when the lesson can reach the fact from what is held. Place a foundation when it can't. In the prompts these are Principle ii and Principle i (`teacher/05-method.md`).

## 7. Optimal learning is two independent optimizations

### 7a. The learner's effort goes into the material

Mental effort per session is finite. Optimize its **proportion**, not its amount.

- Maximize the share in direct contact with the material: following steps, answering checks, holding new facts.
- Minimize logistics: figuring out what to learn, in what order and from what source, researching, fact-checking, planning, finding resources, judging reliability.

Do *not* reduce effort: struggle with the material is how it locks in. All effort goes into the material. The system does the logistics, out of band or silently, never the learner.

### 7b. The minimal tree

A lesson crosses a dependency structure of facts that require other facts. Optimize for **the minimal tree**: the smallest set of propositions, in the smallest derivation depth, from the current understanding to the goal.

- Never re-teach what the learner holds. Measuring prunes those branches.
- Never teach what is off the path to the goal, however interesting. Coverage is not the goal.
- Search the route in planning, never improvise it while teaching. Planning is the highest-leverage work in the session and decides lesson quality: scope the field, find what the goal depends on, prune what is held, choose the route. The plan shown to the learner is visible evidence of that search.

The plan the learner sees is a **lower-resolution view** of this tree: a coarse structural map, not the literal reasoning steps. Plan nodes do not match reasoning steps or turns one to one. The reasoning step is the fine unit crossed in teaching, and the plan tree is the landscape it crosses. The plan is the map, not the road, so never divide the lesson into "Node N" chapters.

## 8. Presentation: the clearest form of each step

Choose the presentation **per step**, as a means to clarity, never a ritual, so each step lands as self-evident.

- State explicitly where best. Unconditional truths are often best asserted plainly ("this is true; take it").
- Weave most connective tissue into the flow instead of announcing it. "Now I will establish X, and then connect it to Y" makes the learner process the *lesson's choreography* instead of the material, which is logistics the theory says to remove. Each fact should arrive as the obvious consequence of the last.
- Avoid **verbosity**, not explicitness: performing the method (labeled segments, restated structure, narrating the method) instead of doing the work. Every sentence installs understanding or is overhead, and overhead must earn its place.

The measure: *does this maximize the learner's absorption?*

## 9. Checks after every reasoning step

Every reasoning step ends with a check: a graded question verifying the step landed. This is the error-catching mechanism of Section 5. The check locates a step that didn't land, and it is strengthened before anything is built on it. Never teach several steps and then check once.

Rhythm: predict (a prediction question can precede a step), step, check (pauses for the learner's answer), then repair or continue.

## 10. What this asks of the system

Measuring must produce precise states, with edges and a concrete goal, because its precision is the ceiling on planning quality. The system owns the logistics (7a), plans for real (7b), teaches in single checked steps (5, 9), and delivers efficiently, explicit only where it helps absorption (8).
