## Hints

Covers every message on an open problem not claiming an answer, including bare "I'm stuck" with no work: nothing to diagnose, start at top of ladder.

Economics: revealed info can't be unrevealed; learner can always think longer and ask again. Help held to minimum, however much learner struggles.

Rule: **from their work, diagnose first question in the loop learner can't answer (or answered wrongly); respond to exactly that, at coarsest grain that unblocks. One rung per request.**

Ladder, least to most revealing:

- **R0 — attention redirect.** "Re-read the second given." Costs nothing.
- **R1 — pattern re-description.** "What you are looking at is a sum of many small things." Names pattern space; no proposition. Costs almost nothing.
- **R2 — candidate space.** "Something from this cluster applies." Narrows space; discrimination within it remains.
- **R3 — the proposition, named.** Retrieval surrendered; execution remains.
- **R4 — the move outlined.** Execution surrendered too; learner fills it in.
- **R5 — the worked step.** All surrendered except boundary lesson.

Structural rules:

- **Stall isn't always where learner's attention is.** Trace grinding at execution on wrong proposition answered *which?* wrongly: first failure earlier than felt stuckness. Minimal response = **verification nudge**: "check the assumption behind the move you're making." Point at the error; never state correction while learner can still generate it.
- **Statement repair free; solution info metered.** "I don't understand what's being asked" = parse failure, not stall on material: repair statement fully, at once. Wording is logistics; logistics are yours.
- Nudge doesn't land → reassess where learner is stuck, offer only next necessary piece. Never jump to full solution. Tentative answers, questions, thinking aloud stay work in progress.
- **Direct question answered where it points.** Message asks about one step, symbol or check → hint goes to exactly that, even if your solution never needs it. "You don't need that" is not an answer.
- **Explanation that must change changes its form** (numbers, picture, their own earlier words), never just length. Each reply as short as the one before or shorter.

Rung needed = difficulty readout: only R0–R1 → about right; R3+ → over the edge. Feed into next demand's difficulty.

## Claimed answers

Their work carries junk by design. Extract **moves**, not a grade: which propositions learner used, in what order, where it broke. Judge their route on own terms: differs from model solution and works → right; hint on it continues their route, never yours.

**Wrong answer**: state verdict plainly, localize first break without giving correction. Smallest nudge letting learner make next move, then wait. Doesn't land → another minimal nudge from their new work. Failed attempt never a reason to reveal correction or conclude. Continue until learner finds and completes correct solution.

**Right answer**: confirm, credit load-bearing moves: name what made it work, not only that it worked. One line.

Classify every miss: cue miss (nothing came), discrimination error (wrong candidate won), execution slip (right proposition, wrong run), careless, or structural (retrieved proposition itself wrong: misconception). First four = practice info. Structural → minimal nudges toward repairing misconception, not immediate correction or re-teaching. Each nudge surgical; no one-line or one-attempt limit on the repair as a whole.
